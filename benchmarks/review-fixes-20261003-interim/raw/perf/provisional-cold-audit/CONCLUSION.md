# What another cold run would add

The existing eight complete paired blocks provide useful cold evidence now. They cover all 66 native configurations and all three variants, including four fonts, natural and rounded text positions, a fresh 94-glyph singleton, two phases, 32 distinct sizes, 32 normalized variable-font instances, and the complete 67-frame pollution sequence. No additional cold run is required to observe the broad benefit: all 66 final/master paired point ratios favor final; 63 exploratory 95% intervals are below one, three cross one, and none are wholly above one.

The remaining uncertainty is limited. For default features plus Swash, the Vollkorn 32-size sequence has paired ratio 0.99470, interval [0.99236, 1.01068]. Its complete-sequence delta is −252.021 us, interval [−365.917, +507.625] (−7.876 us/frame, interval [−11.435, +15.863]). Default-plus-Swash Arial one-phase labels and paragraphs have delta intervals reaching +9.042 and +4.750 us respectively. These intervals permit small costs; they do not establish a slowdown. A further clean cold cohort would mainly tighten those intervals and strengthen environmental provenance.

This is an explicitly post hoc provisional analysis of the chronologically first interrupted campaign, selected before calculating these cold results. It is not the original planned 12-block clean primary study. The process-name guard ran before configurations and could miss brief background work during a process. A later detected build does not prove every earlier process was contaminated, nor does it prove the earlier interval was continuously idle. These limitations remain in the report and must accompany any use of the numbers.

The controlled costs include population, all distinct-instance requests, pollution and return. The pollution total cannot isolate hot-return latency or prove actual eviction; it does show that the complete pressure sequence has no observed net cost at these point estimates. The committed correctness tests separately cover cache retention, eviction and budget behavior.

The primary twelve-block warm/generic evidence remains unchanged, including the four no-Swash warm paragraph increases of 1.313–2.4165 us/frame and the other exploratory positive intervals. This cold reanalysis does not erase those costs. Fresh demo/text scene data would answer a separate question about full CPU scene drawing including layout; it would not make the existing cold matrix more complete in workload coverage.
